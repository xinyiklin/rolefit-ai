Browser extension

Import job postings with one click.

Use the RoleFit extension in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox to send the posting you are reading into your local workspace, ready to prepare. Open the popup, or press ⌘⇧UCommand plus Shift plus U /or Ctrl+Shift+UControl plus Shift plus U to import without it.

  • Chrome
  • Firefox

Local-first and your privacy

Your workspace stays on your device.

RoleFit runs as a desktop companion that opens the workbench in your browser. Your workspace stays on this machine. When you run an AI step, only the input it needs goes directly to the provider you chose. There is no hosted backend, sync service, or telemetry.
