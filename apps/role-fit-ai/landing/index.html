Application workflow

Prepare, export, and track one application.

Prepare the posting, work on both documents, save the files you plan to send, and keep dates and follow-up with the application record.

01 · cover letter

Polish the letter, then accept or discard the proposal.

Open a saved variant or your own template. RoleFit checks the proposed revision against resume evidence and leaves the current letter unchanged until you accept it. Save the editable result as a .cover file or export a PDF.

02 · resume

Create one grounded resume proposal.

Polish selected sections from Resume or Prepare. Apply all or accept, edit, and discard individual changes while the source resume stays under your control.

03 · track + follow up

Save the application record and its follow-up.

Store stage, fit verdict, dates, next action, notes, and saved artifacts in the same local workspace.
